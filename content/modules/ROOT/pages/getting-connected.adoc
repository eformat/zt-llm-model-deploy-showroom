= Getting Connected

In this activity, you will connect to the OpenShift console, open the Red Hat OpenShift AI dashboard, and confirm your assigned project is ready for the workshop.

== Login to OpenShift

. Open the OpenShift console in your browser:
+
{openshift_console_url}

. Log in with the following credentials:
+
**Username:** `{user}`
+
**Password:** `{password}`
+
image::01-keycloak-login.png[Login page,link=self,window=blank,width=700]

. Once logged in, you should see the OpenShift console Projects page.

== Open the RHOAI dashboard

. In the top navigation bar, click the grid icon (application launcher).

. In the dropdown, under **OpenShift Self Managed Services**, click **Red Hat OpenShift AI**.
+
The RHOAI dashboard opens in a new browser tab. It may take a few seconds to load.
+
image::02-rhoai-dashboard-home.png[RHOAI Dashboard Home,link=self,window=blank,width=700]

== Confirm your assigned project

Your lab environment comes with a data science project that already has a GPU/MIG hardware profile enabled. You'll deploy the model into this project in Module 2.

. In the left sidebar, click **Projects**.

. Confirm you can see your assigned project, `{project_name}`, in the list.
+
image::03-projects-list.png[Projects list,link=self,window=blank,width=700]

You can also verify from the command line:

[source,bash,role="execute",subs="attributes"]
----
oc get project {project_name}
----

== End of activity

You are logged in, the RHOAI dashboard is open, and your GPU-backed project is ready. Continue to **Module 1** to discover and benchmark a model.
